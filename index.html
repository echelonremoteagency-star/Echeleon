Founder & Operations Lead

Masheica Thomas

Masheica Thomas is the Founder and Operations Lead of Echelon Remote, a growing operational support agency dedicated to helping businesses build stronger systems, improve communication, and streamline day-to-day operations. With a passion for organization, workflow management, and professional client support, she established Echelon Remote to provide dependable operational solutions for entrepreneurs, executives, businesses, and creative professionals. Under her leadership, Echelon Remote has expanded beyond traditional virtual assistance to include executive support, recruitment coordination, social media management, workflow systems, AI-powered operational solutions, and specialized support for artists and creative professionals. Her mission is to help clients focus on growth while Echelon Remote provides the structure, systems, and operational support needed to sustain long-term success. The agency also embraces global collaboration, working with talented remote professionals across multiple regions and time zones to deliver flexible and scalable support solutions.
